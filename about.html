About KM Cleaning Services

Professional cleaning with integrity and excellence

Our Story

KM Cleaning Services was founded in 2014 with a simple mission: to provide exceptional cleaning services that exceed customer expectations. What started as a small operation with just two dedicated professionals has grown into a trusted cleaning company serving hundreds of satisfied clients across Nairobi and surrounding regions of Kenya.

Our founder, with over 15 years of experience in the cleaning industry, recognized a gap in the market for reliable, professional, and affordable cleaning services. We built our business on the principles of transparency, quality, and customer care—values that remain central to everything we do today.

Today, KM Cleaning Services operates a team of trained, vetted, and professional cleaners who are passionate about transforming spaces and creating clean, healthy environments for our clients throughout Kenya.
